07 Account and Data Deletion

Hishab Potro does not create or maintain conventional user accounts or store your ledger records on Apdroid System's own servers. Google OAuth is used for Google account access and related features.

If you want to remove all data associated with your use of Hishab Potro, you are responsible for completing the following steps:

  1. Delete local App data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Hishab Potro → Storage → Clear Data.
  2. Delete your Google Drive backup: Open Hishab Potro → Settings → Backup Settings → Delete Cloud Backup.
  3. Revoke Google account access: Visit your Google Account permissions page and remove Hishab Potro.
  4. Uninstall the App: Uninstall Hishab Potro from your Android device.

Clearing local data removes records stored on your device. Deleting the cloud backup removes the encrypted backup from your Google Drive app folder. Revoking Google access removes the App's OAuth authorization.

Certain anonymized Firebase Analytics events and AdMob information may remain with Google according to Google's own data retention policies. Apdroid System does not control the retention or deletion of information held by those third-party services.

If you need help with the deletion process, contact apdroidsystem.dev@gmail.com .
